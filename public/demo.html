How to judge her

Run the calls you actually get. The three bedroom cleanout, the couch on the curb, the price shopper, the how much for a truckload. Then throw a couple of the odd ones at her, the stuff that comes up once a month.

You're not testing whether she can be broken. Anyone can break anyone on day one. You're deciding whether she can run your phone. Those are different tests and only one of them tells you anything.

So judge her the way you'd judge a receptionist in their first week. The Caroline you're about to hear is the worst she'll ever be. She'll stumble now and then, it's rare, and every call makes her better. What she'll never do is miss a call, call in sick, or get short with a customer at 8pm on a Saturday.

Your demo line is live for 24 hours, then she clocks out and you'll get our pricing to bring her on for real. After each call, check your email: she sends you the recording.
