# Spec: Shane's calendar behind demo completion

Approved by Lee 2026-08-29. For Cale. Small change, stated precisely so it is not
misread as "add a link somewhere."

## The rule

**The Calendly link is shown only to a lead who has COMPLETED a demo.** Never before,
in any surface, at any stage.

    completed a demo     -> Calendly link, in the follow up and on the post demo screen
    did not complete     -> email nurture only, no Calendly link in any message

## What "completed a demo" means

Not "started" and not "requested." The lead must have got through the flow to the point
of the demo call actually happening. Cale owns the exact field; the intent is that the
person heard Jenny answer as their company.

If that state is not currently recorded on the lead, **recording it is part of this
change**, because the gate cannot exist without it. This overlaps the funnel counters
David is building (demos started / calls placed / booked) and should reuse the same
signal rather than inventing a second one.

## Why the link must be absent rather than disabled

Do not render the link greyed out, behind a message, or with a "complete your demo
first" note. **Absent.** A visible-but-blocked booking link reads as a gate the reader
is being asked to earn, which is a worse experience than simply not being offered a
call yet.

## What does NOT change

Nothing about the ad, the targeting, the budget, or the demo form itself. Nobody is
excluded from the funnel; everybody still gets email. The only difference is who is
offered Shane's time.

## Why

Shane is the one part of this funnel that does not scale. Email scales infinitely, ads
scale with budget, the demo scales with servers. **You cannot buy more Shane.** A demo
costs the owner real effort (company, city, service area, their own pricing, a verified
cell, an actual inbound call), so completing one is the strongest qualification signal
we have, and it is one we already collect.

This costs zero leads. Everyone we would have sent to Shane is still in the funnel; we
have simply not spent Shane on them yet.
