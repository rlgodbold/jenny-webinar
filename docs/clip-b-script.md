# Clip B script: "Her, in your own voice"

Target file: public/clips/her-in-your-voice.mp3
This is the last go-live blocker on demo.html. The section renders itself the moment
the file exists.

## The design decision: make it an A/B, not a sample

One clip, two halves. **The same fifteen seconds of call, twice.** First in the
standard voice the prospect just heard on their own demo call, then the identical
exchange in Lee's cloned voice.

Three reasons this beats a standalone sample:

1. **It isolates the variable.** A clip of a good voice proves nothing, because they
   have no reference. The same words twice makes the voice the only thing that
   changed, which is the entire claim.
2. **It cannot be mistaken for a real customer call.** Nobody believes a customer
   called twice and said the same thing. That matters, because a synthetic call
   presented on a page full of real capability could otherwise read as proof, and
   proof has a consent bar this does not clear.
3. **It ties back to their own experience.** They just heard the standard voice live,
   minutes earlier. The first half is familiar, so the second half lands as a change
   to something they know rather than a claim about something they do not.

## Script, about fifteen seconds per half

    CALLER   Yeah, I've got a couch and a mattress I need gone.

    JENNY    Got it, a couch and a mattress. Are those coming from inside the house
             or are they out in the garage?

    CALLER   Garage.

    JENNY    Easy. That's about a quarter load. I've got tomorrow morning between
             nine and eleven, want me to hold it for you?

No dollar figure. Junk pricing is per client and a number here belongs to nobody, so
it would either be wrong or read as ours. "About a quarter load" is how the estimate
actually works and it demonstrates the same competence without inventing a price.

Nothing in this exchange claims a dark capability. She answers, she asks the one
question that changes the estimate, she quotes a load fraction, she offers a window.
Answer, quote, book, which is exactly the three verbs.

## Production notes

- Both halves must be the identical script, identical pacing, identical caller audio
  if possible. Any other difference gives the ear something else to notice.
- Consent is clean: it is Lee's own voice and he is the only person who can consent
  to himself. Do not substitute a client's cloned voice to make it more impressive.
- Existing Dale material is probably the wrong source. It was recorded for a different
  purpose, and the A/B only works if both halves are this script. A purpose built pair
  is about ten minutes of work and it is worth doing properly.
- Keep it under 35 seconds total. This is a capability demonstration sitting below the
  main event, not the main event.
