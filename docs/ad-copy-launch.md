# Launch ad copy

Four videos, all cut from Lee's single 2 minute take. Copy below is the text that
sits above the video in the feed, plus headline and button.

## Claim rules these obey

Say: she answers every call, quotes your prices, books the job into your Workiz,
cancels and reschedules, handles the awkward calls (hot tubs, estate cleanouts),
optional custom voice, live in days.

Do NOT say: live transfer, owner texting, emergency escalation, any CRM other than
Workiz. All four are cut from the video and none appear in the copy.

---

## PRIMARY TEXT A, the trap (default, pairs with any cut)

    You always hire the phone person at the worst possible moment.

    It's 97 degrees, you're on a truck, you're behind on estimates, and the phone
    will not stop. So that's the week you're supposed to write a job post,
    interview six people, hire one, and train them on your pricing.

    That's how you end up rushing a hire you should have taken your time on. They
    quit or you let them go, and four months later you do it again.

    I built the one that shows up ready. She answers every call, quotes your
    prices, and books the job straight into your Workiz.

    Hear her answer a real call.

## PRIMARY TEXT B, short (for the 60 second cuts)

    The day you finally admit you can't answer the phone anymore is the worst
    possible day to hire somebody to do it.

    She answers every call, quotes your prices, and books the job into your
    Workiz. No interviewing, no payroll, no training her at ten at night.

    Hear her answer a real call.

## PRIMARY TEXT C, proof first (test against A)

    19 calls yesterday. She booked real jobs, and not one caller asked if she was
    AI. If they had, she'd have told them.

    She answers every call, quotes your prices, and books straight into your
    Workiz. You can even have her use a cloned voice from someone in your own
    market.

    Hear her answer a real call.

## HEADLINES, test two

    The hire that shows up ready
    She answers every call. You get your evening back.

## DESCRIPTION

    Answers, quotes, and books into your Workiz.

## BUTTON

    Learn More

Not "Sign Up" and not "Get Offer." Nothing is being signed up for at the click, and
Learn More sets an accurate expectation for a page that asks them to hear a call.

---

## THE FOUR VIDEOS

    A1   1:41   Full story, both halves. Best for retargeting and warm audiences.
    B    1:03   Trap plus the reveal. The cold traffic default.
    C    1:03   Same as B, hook reads "You're about to hire the wrong person"
    D    1:03   Same as B, hook reads "The worst time to hire is when you need to"

B, C and D are identical except for the on-screen hook in the first 3.6 seconds.
That is the cleanest test we can run off one shoot: same everything, one variable.
Whichever hook wins gets rebuilt onto A1 for the retargeting audience.

## Upload notes

**Choose the thumbnail manually.** Meta defaults to frame one, which is Lee looking
down with the hat brim across his face. Any frame from about ten seconds on is
better. This is the single highest leverage thirty seconds of the upload.

**Captions are burned in**, so no caption file is needed and none should be added.
Most of these views play muted and the burned captions are doing the work.

**Placements:** Reels, Stories and Feed. The footage is 9:16 and fills the frame.
Captions sit high enough to clear the bottom UI on Reels.
