# JRA marketing plan, skeleton

For Alston's gate, then Lee. Built on CLAIM-FREE assets only, per the interim rule.
Nothing here proposes spend that has not been named as a proposal. Nothing has been built.

Companion doc: `jra-discovery-map.md` holds the evidence behind every claim made here.

---

## THE SHAPE OF THE OPPORTUNITY

The instinct with a "grow leads" mandate is to go buy traffic. On this account that would be
the wrong first move, and the discovery says so plainly:

**The three largest available gains cost nothing in media.**

    Contact Center has no sales page   a product in the nav and on /services/ that dead ends
    STB cannot be measured at all      no call tracking, no analytics, no pixel, one static number
    The blog stopped in Aug 2023       on a site that sells SEO as a product

Every one of those leaks traffic JRA is already earning. **Buying more traffic before fixing
them means paying to fill a bucket with holes in it.** That is the sequence argument and it
is the spine of this plan.

## SEGMENT BY MOMENT, NOT BY PRODUCT

Everything JRA sells goes to one person: a junk removal business owner. What changes is
where they are.

    MOMENT                     WHAT THEY WANT              PRODUCT
    thinking about starting    a way in without a franchise  Start Up Package
    open but not growing       leads                        SEO / Google Ads / website
    growing, drowning in calls  their evenings back          THE PHONE LADDER (undecided)
    growing, out of capacity   trucks                       Specialty Truck Bodies
    quoting badly              faster accurate quotes       Video Job Estimator

Two consequences the plan is built on:

**One content engine, one lead pool.** A lead who is wrong for one product is often right for
another, so the job is to capture the owner and route by moment, not to run six campaigns.

**Never present the catalogue.** An owner who sees six things for sale trusts each one less.
Each entry point sells one thing; the rest is discovered later.

## PHASE 1: FIX THE LEAKS (no media spend, largest return per hour)

Specced by me, approved by Lee, built by whoever owns the site. **None by me.**

**1. The Contact Center decision.** Not a fix, a question: is it sold? If yes it needs a
sales page; if no it comes out of the nav and the /services/ index. Either way the redirect
to a thank-you page for a form nobody submitted is wrong and should go to /services/.

**2. Instrument Specialty Truck Bodies.** Call tracking first, via the CallRail master
account that already exists, because on six of eight pages the phone IS the conversion. Then
analytics and a pixel. Then a form on /trucks/ and /financing/. **Until this is done STB
cannot be advertised**, because the spend could never be evaluated.

**3. The blog.** Two years dormant on a site selling SEO. Decide: revive or retire. A stale
blog is a credibility problem before it is a traffic problem, and prospects who are buying
SEO from you will look.

## PHASE 2: JRA PAID, THE READY TARGET

JRA moves first because it is already instrumented and every service page has a form. This
is a reversal of my earlier guess that STB was the better paid target: STB has the better
ticket, JRA has the working plumbing.

**The template already exists.** The Jenny campaign built this week is the pattern: pixel,
verified domain, custom audiences, a lookalike from real buyers, UTM discipline, and a
landing page that names its audience in the first six words. Reuse it rather than reinvent.

**Entry points, one per moment**, each to its own landing page and its own ad set:

    starting out    ->  Start Up Package     the franchise-alternative story, Lee's own
    needs leads     ->  marketing services   proof-led, their competitors are outranking them
    drowning        ->  the phone ladder     GATED on Lee's ladder decision

**Creative.** The claim-free testimonial cuts are the strongest asset available, subject to
the human claim log. Six real named owners on camera beats anything we could write.

**Budget.** I am proposing no number until close rates exist. Naming a budget before knowing
what a lead is worth is guessing dressed as planning. The moment lead value per line is
known, budget follows from it mechanically.

## PHASE 3: SPECIALTY TRUCK BODIES, AFTER INSTRUMENTATION

Higher ticket, longer consideration, better testimonials, and **probably capacity capped**.
If STB can build N trucks a month, the goal is not "grow leads", it is "generate exactly
enough and stop." That is a completely different campaign and the number decides it.

Ads point at **junkremovaltrucksforsale.com**, the canonical domain.

## MEASUREMENT, WITH ALSTON

Campaign side is mine, dashboard is hers. Three non-negotiables:

**Close rate per product line, never blended.** If one person can buy six things, a single
JRA close rate hides everything worth knowing.

**Attribution captured at the moment of the lead.** UTM values, source, and landing page
recorded on the lead record itself. This cannot be reconstructed later, which is why it must
exist before the first dollar rather than after the first report.

**A lead-to-sale join.** Cost per lead alone cannot tell the difference between bad traffic,
a broken funnel, and a working funnel that does not sell. Those have three different fixes,
and choosing wrong is expensive. This needs the CRM, which I still cannot see.

## WHAT I NEED FROM LEE, in the order the plan needs them

1. **Is the phone a ladder?** Train the person, outsource the person, replace the person.
   One offer at three moments, or three products competing. This also answers the Contact
   Center question, because they are the same question.
2. **Price and margin per line.** Lead value decides budget. Without it any number I give is
   invented.
3. **Where do JRA sales live, and can I read them?** No CRM join, no close rates, and close
   rates are what he explicitly asked for.
4. **How many trucks can STB build per month?** Decides whether STB is a growth campaign or
   a capped one.
5. **Is the blog dormant on purpose?**
6. **The affiliate program: what is it, is it active, and what are the real terms?** Possibly
   replaces the agency-partnership work he asked me for.

## WHAT I WILL NOT DO

Propose a budget before lead value is known. Put spend behind income claims before counsel
clears them. Touch the live site. Design a campaign around an asset that cannot run.
