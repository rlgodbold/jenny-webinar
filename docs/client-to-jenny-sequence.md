# THREE THINGS TO SETTLE BEFORE THIS SENDS

**1. [MISSED_REVENUE] is an income claim and needs handling.** A dollar figure asserted to a
client about money they lost is exactly the class of claim I flagged across JRA's live site.
**I have written it so the arithmetic is visible and conservative rather than asserted**: the
copy says "if even a quarter of those were real jobs, at your average ticket that is roughly
X." That makes the assumption inspectable and the number defensible. If the merge value is
computed some other way, the sentence has to change with it. **Do not let anyone swap in a
bare "you lost $X last month."**

**2. Confirm the year, and write it.** The brief says "through March 31" and "before April 1."
Today is August 2026, so I have written **March 31, 2027** and **April 1, 2027**. That is a
seven month discount window, which is generous, so please confirm it is what Lee means and not
a shorter one. Richard's standing rule is that every offer date carries its year.

**3. The refund line needs adjusting because setup is waived.** The standard terms say "Cancel
in the first 30 days and we refund the monthly charge. Setup fee is non refundable." **There
is no setup fee here**, so that sentence does not apply as written. I have adjusted it. Terms
wording is normally byte identical across surfaces, so this exception should be deliberate and
noted rather than looking like drift.

**One structural note.** These people already pay JRA. The single biggest risk is that this
reads as being cross sold, which costs trust on everything else JRA sells them. So touch 1
opens with their number and their situation, and the offer arrives late and quietly. **If it
reads like a promotion we have already lost.**

# WHAT I DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT

**No testimonials and no results claims.** They are quarantined pending counsel, and this
sequence does not need them: the reader's own missed call number is stronger proof than
somebody else's success story, and the demo settles the rest.

**No specific lead lift or ranking claim.** The LSA point is true and documented, but it is a
different conversation and adding it here would dilute a message that is already working from
their own data.

**No urgency beyond the real one.** The April 1, 2027 price change is a genuine deadline with
a real number attached. I have not invented seats, cohorts or scarcity, and I would push back
on adding any.
